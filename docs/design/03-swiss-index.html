Index

One typeface, one grid, one accent. Content is ordered by position and size, never by boxes. Numbers are large because numbers are the argument.

01Principles

The grid does the grouping.

Twelve columns, flush left. Alignment replaces cards, borders and shadows.

Size is hierarchy.

Big numbers for measures, a large ask field, small grey labels. No weight is used for decoration.

Red is a point, not a surface.

A 10 px square marks the active item. Teal and amber squares mark current and replaced.
